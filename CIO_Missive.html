How The Process Works

Measure the economy

Growth, inflation, labor, credit, fiscal policy, housing, manufacturing, consumers, and small business data determine whether the economy is helping or hurting risk-taking.

Step 2

Check the market tape

ETF trend, breadth, drawdown pressure, credit, rates, positioning, options, and momentum decide whether markets agree with the economic story.

Step 3

Translate into portfolio actions

The CIO layer converts the evidence into add, hold, wait, trim, cash, and hedge decisions.

Step 4

Drill down to tickers

Individual ticker pages show the proof behind specific ETF decisions, including trend, breadth, options, DeMark, holdings, and cross-deck agreement.

Refresh order: source decks, market and economy command centers, then CIO. The CIO command refreshes the Quad first, writes the missive, and exports ticker intelligence using the same decision snapshot.
