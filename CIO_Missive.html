Strategist Group Score Definitions

These definitions explain the group-score table at right without pushing the table lower on the page. Current health-source mix: Price proxy: 98 ETFs, Breadth: 15 ETFs.

Strategy ScoreRelative sleeve rank

Ranks which ETF groups deserve portfolio attention now. It blends Health, short-term return momentum, Trend Breadth, inverse Damage Breadth, and relative-strength/extension z-score overlays. It is a relative market leadership score, not a 0-100 macro gauge.

HealthBreadth quality

Average ETF health inside the group. Where full breadth data exists, this comes from the strategist breadth-health engine. Where breadth data is unavailable, the fallback price proxy is intentionally smoother: 45% trend, 40% 1W/1M/3M return rank, and 15% drawdown relief.

Price Proxy HealthFallback for ETFs without breadth data

The proxy now gives more weight to trend and return confirmation and less weight to drawdown relief, so a normal pullback or rough day does not tighten the model as aggressively.

TrendShare in uptrends

Average trend breadth for the group. Higher means more ETFs are above key moving-average and trend thresholds, so the advance is broader.

DamageShare with technical damage

Average damage breadth for the group. Higher is worse. For price-proxy ETFs, drawdown damage only starts once the ETF is more than 10% below its trailing 1-year high; deeper drawdowns then scale by magnitude and rank.

Leadership ReadPM action translation

Plain-English bucket derived from rank, Strategy Score, Health, and Damage. Use it to separate groups to add, watch, or avoid before drilling into individual ETFs.

Portfolio Translation

The CIO Missive uses the command centers to translate data into risk, sleeve, cash, and timing decisions. Final Model Weight is read against Base Weight; Weight Delta is the direct over/underweight instruction. Cash / Dry Powder is included so the reserve bucket cannot disappear from the CIO read.

Overlay construction: Overlay equals Positioning + VAMS + FCI + Options after each sleeve's sensitivity multiplier is applied. Positioning comes from CFTC equity-index crowding or washout only; SOFR-3M and Treasury futures are treated as rates/funding context for FCI, MOVE, duration, and credit interpretation. VAMS comes from the volatility-adjusted momentum buy-rate and fresh buy/sell balance, FCI comes from the financial-conditions score plus its 4-week change, and Options comes from validation-aware forward-return setup, PM Action, movement risk, protection demand, hedge urgency, gamma stability, and upside chase.
